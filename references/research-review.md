Not adopted: fake personal details, mandatory slang, blanket punctuation bans, arbitrary rhythm thresholds, detector-evasion claims, or transmission to remote humanization services. No upstream implementation or examples were copied into the V2 additions. Retained legacy material keeps its original MIT attribution.
